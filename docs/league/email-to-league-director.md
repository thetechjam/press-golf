# Email to the league director

Draft for introducing Press to the league before the season. Attach
`press-league-flyer.pdf`. Names are left as placeholders because this repo is
public.

**Subject:** A free scoring app for Thursday league — built to our rule sheet

Hi [League director],

I've been building a free golf scoring app called Press, and I've set up a
league mode that follows our [2025] rule sheet. I wanted to run it by you
before sharing it with anyone.

It handles:

- The A and B singles plus the better-ball team match, a point each
- Strokes off the low handicap in the foursome, one a hole and no more than 9
  in a match
- Low handicaps matched against each other
- A max score of 9 on any hole, and an X for a pick-up
- The darkness/lightning rule after 5 holes, including resuming on a later day
- A team playing one player short (forfeit + team point)
- New players and subs at 70% until they've played 3 matches, then 90%

One person in the foursome keeps score on their phone and it shows who's up in
every match as they go. It's free, there's no account or download, and it works
with no signal. It doesn't replace anything: players still sign and hand in
their cards, and your spreadsheet stays the official record.

Would you be OK with me mentioning it at a league night, or putting a small
flyer in the pro shop? I've attached one. And if you spot anything that doesn't
match how you run the league, I'd genuinely like to know so I can fix it.

You can try it at pressgolf.netlify.app. Tap "Golf League".

Thanks,
[Your name]
