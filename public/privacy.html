Privacy

Press keeps score for golf side games. It has no accounts, no analytics and no server that stores your rounds. This page says exactly what the app does with your data, in plain English. It matches the code; if the code changes, this page changes with it.

Where your data lives

Everything Press saves — rounds, scores, players' names and handicaps, stakes, saved courses, merged-name records and your settings — is stored in your browser, on your device, in the browser's local storage for pressgolf.netlify.app. Nothing is copied to a server. Press asks the browser to treat that storage as persistent so it is not cleared when space is tight; installing Press to your home screen is the surest way to keep it.

There are no cookies, no tracking pixels, no third-party scripts and no usage analytics. The app's own files are cached on your device by a service worker so it opens with no signal.

What leaves your device, and when

Nothing leaves your device unless you do one of these things:

  • Course search. When you type three or more characters into the course field on New Round, that text is sent to api.opengolfapi.org to look up matching courses, and picking a result fetches that course's scorecard from the same service. Course data is © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL 1.0) via OpenGolfAPI. Nothing about you or your round is sent — only what you typed.
  • Sending feedback. Settings → Send feedback posts what you write — the report, and your name if you give one — together with a few diagnostics (which screen you were on, the app version and build, your browser's user-agent string, the screen size and the time) to Netlify Forms, the hosting company's form service, which emails it to the developer. The round you are on is included only if you tick "Include this round", which is off every time you open the form; ticking it sends the players' names, scores and settings. A report written with no signal waits on your device and is sent when you are back online.
  • Sharing a round by link or QR code. The whole round — names, scores, handicaps, stakes, presses, junk — is packed into the link itself, after the #. Browsers never send that part of a link to any server, including ours, so the round goes only to whoever you give the link to. The link is compressed, not encrypted: anyone who has it can open it and read the names, scores and stakes inside, and it will sit in the history of whatever app you sent it through. Share a link the way you would share the scorecard itself.
  • Sharing an image or text. The scoreboard and scorecard images are drawn on your device and handed to your phone's share sheet, or saved as a file. Where they go from there is up to you and the app you pick.
  • Backups. Back up from Settings writes a plain JSON file containing every round, saved course and merged-name record on the device. It is not encrypted. You keep it; restoring one merges it back.
  • Links to other sites. The tutorial (YouTube), the tip jar (Buy Me a Coffee) and the OpenStreetMap credit are ordinary links that open those sites, which have their own privacy policies. Nothing is sent to them until you tap.

Press is served by Netlify. Like any website, loading or updating the app sends ordinary requests for its files, which the host sees as it would for any site (your IP address, your browser). Press adds nothing to those requests and reads nothing back from them.

Deleting your data

  • Rounds: Rounds → Select, pick the ones to remove, delete. Or the × on a single round's card.
  • Saved courses: New Round → Course → the × beside a saved course.
  • Feedback waiting to send: Settings → Clear waiting reports.
  • Everything at once: Settings → Erase everything. The first tap says what goes; the second removes every round, saved course, player name, feedback waiting to send and setting Press has stored, and the app opens again as it did the first time. The same result without opening the app: clear the site's data in your browser (on iPhone, Settings → Safari → Advanced → Website Data; on Android Chrome, the site's page info → Site settings → Delete data), or uninstall the installed app.

A round that someone else sent you by link is not saved until you tap "Keep it".
